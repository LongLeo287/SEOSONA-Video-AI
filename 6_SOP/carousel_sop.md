# SOP: SEOSONA Carousel Design

*Aligned with SEOSONA Tech-Editorial Brand Identity*

> **Related, deliberately SEPARATE:** [`thumbnail_sop.md`](thumbnail_sop.md) shares the SAME brand palette
> (navy #1A2DB5 · blue #1565C0 · yellow #FFD54F · Be Vietnam Pro) but is a DISTINCT deliverable: carousel
> = multi-slide post (cover + content slides, `carousel_maker`); thumbnail = single video-cover image
> (`thumbnail_maker.make_thumbnail`). Shared palette ≠ duplicate; not merged.

## 1. Specifications
- **Size Options**: 1080×1080 (Square) OR 1080×1920 (Vertical for TikTok/Reels) — stick to one per carousel.
- **Style**: Minimalist, Professional, Tech-editorial, emphasizing white space.

## 2. Standard Color Palette
- **#1A2DB5 (Dark Navy)**: Primary accent, Cover slide background.
- **#1565C0 (Blue)**: Accent text, Labels, Icons.
- **#BBDEFB (Light Blue)**: Text on Navy background to highlight keywords.
- **#F3F6FA (Light Background)**: Background for content slides.
- **#0E1633 (Ink)**: Main text color.
- **#5A6588 (Gray)**: Secondary text color.
- **#E2E8F2 (Border)**: Thin border lines.
- **#FFD54F (Yellow)**: STRICTLY to highlight 1-2 key phrases on Navy background.

## 3. Typography & Assets
- **Font**: Be Vietnam Pro (or Geometric Sans-serif).
- **Titles**: Bold, tight line-height.
- **Body Text**: Regular, spacious line-height of 1.5.
- **Icons**: Thin Line/Outline (Stroke ~1.7px), rounded caps, monochromatic Blue. NO emojis, NO 3D/colorful icons.

## 4. System Layout
- **Global Background**: Light `#F3F6FA` with very subtle dot grid + 1 giant, faded typographic/numeric watermark in the corner.
- **Global Header**: SEOSONA Logo in the top-left corner on all slides.
- **Global Footer**: Text "SEOSONA · Share to be shared more" + thin top border line.

### Cover Slide
- **Background**: Solid Navy (#1A2DB5) with subtle white dots.
- **Label**: Rounded pill label (e.g., "TIPS FOR USING...").
- **Title**: Massive size, White color. 1 keyword changed to Light Blue.
- **Accents**: Accent underline, short description, pagination dots at the bottom.

### Content Slides
- **Header**: Huge numbering (01/02/03) in Navy + small uppercase label next to it.
- **Title**: Large title with 1 phrase italicized-and-not-italicized in Blue. Short accent line.
- **Content Blocks**: White panel cards, large rounded corners (~20-30px), thin border, very subtle shadow.
- **Lists**: Use line icons placed inside rounded square containers.
- **Closing Band**: Each slide ends with a Navy rounded "closing band" at the bottom containing an icon + 1 concluding sentence. Highlight key words in Light Blue or Yellow.

## 5. Core Principles
- Minimalist, prioritize white space.
- Max 1-2 background colors per set.
- NO flashy gradients.
- Minimum text size must be large and readable.
- Exactly 1 main idea per slide.
