# SOP: Video Course / Tutorial Production

Mandatory standard operating procedure for all AI Agents and Human Editors producing "Tutorial / Course" videos for SEOSONA / Chi Quyet Academy.

## 1. Iron Rule: B2B EXPERT AESTHETIC (No Dark Mode)

- **Strictly Light Theme:** Dark Mode and dark backgrounds are STRICTLY PROHIBITED for course videos. The content must exude a "B2B Expert", professional, and clean aesthetic.
- **Colors:** Use pristine white backgrounds (`#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC`) with Navy text (`#04091A`).
- **Code Snippets:** Even for coding tutorials, use Light Theme syntax highlighting (e.g., GitHub Light or similar) instead of Dark Mode.

## 2. Format & Layout Standard

- **Aspect Ratio:** Default is strictly 9:16 (1080×1920) vertical.
- **Pacing:** Slower and more educational than fast-paced news videos.
- **Visuals:** Emphasize clear diagrams, readable text, and structured UI mockups.

## 3. Typography & Subtitles

- **Style:** "Anchor" mode — Subtitles must be anchored neatly at the bottom safe zone (bottom 15-20%), cleanly formatted without explosive karaoke animations, suitable for prolonged reading.
- **Font:** Professional sans-serif (`Be Vietnam Pro`), clean and unobtrusive.

## 4. Voice Standard

- **Required Voice:** Default is Male Southern Vietnamese target profile, matching the professional and consultative tone of the brand.

## 5. Final Delivery Gate

- Ensure all requirements from `video_production_sop.md` are met (Auto-Cleanup, `Báo_cáo_chất_lượng.md`, etc.).
