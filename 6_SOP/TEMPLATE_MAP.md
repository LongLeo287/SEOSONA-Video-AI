## Distribution
- **repo-showcase** (light) — a repo/tool with ★: ~6 videos
- **tool-walkthrough** (light) — tool + how to use it: ~6 videos
- **ai-news-flash** (light) — quick news / feature / numbers: ~8 videos
- **resource-list** (light) — collection/list/roundup: ~6 videos
- **insight-explainer** (light) — concept/opinion: ~3 videos
- **tutorial-gittree** (light) — learning/visual: ~3 videos

## Rotation rules (so news never gets stale)
1. Pick by **content type** (template column above).
2. Within the same type, **rotate the accent** (blue→green→coral) between consecutive videos to diversify the feed (all light mode, exact brand colors).
3. Versus/benchmark (Kimi, Gemini SQL2) → `ai-news-flash` + use the `compare` scene for the "face-off" shot.

## Audio (new)

impact when a number appears, keystrokes under the terminal, UI sound on badge/CTA — matching the "produced" feel
of the sample videos. See `7_ASSETS/audio/sfx/manifest.json`.
