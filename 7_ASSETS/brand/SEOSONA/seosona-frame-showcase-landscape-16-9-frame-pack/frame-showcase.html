Principles

do · don't

Atoms are sacred · composition is free · numbers come from the script. The three eyeball tests gate every frame before any structural check.

Do

Lean centered.

Cover, claim, stat, and closer all center their focal element. Left-anchor is reserved for catalog and ledger. No two consecutive frames share an anchor.

Do

One signal per frame.

Blue fires once at full strength — a dot, a pill, a sparkline. The blue-tint status chip doesn't count as the signal firing.

Do

Hairline + whisper.

Depth is one 1px slate-200 border plus a barely-there shadow. Two elevation steps maximum per frame. No glassmorphism.

Don't

No dark grounds.

Source rule. Ink is a chrome-scale surface — a pill, a stat token, a button — never a full-frame ground. No neon. No invented hex.

Don't

No SaaS feature grid.

Three icon+headline columns under a top-left kicker is the deck tell. Break it into a longer sequence of sparser frames. One idea per frame.

Don't

No sharp corners.

Every CTA is a pill. Every card is 28–40px radius. Every chip is full-radius. The brand silhouette is soft.
