- Quality > quantity; warns against chasing tool metrics / Black-Hat.
- For SHORT 9:16 videos: distill ONE idea per video from a transcript; keep the mentor voice,
  drop the webinar filler. Hook → 1 insight → CTA (follow SEOSONA / the course).
