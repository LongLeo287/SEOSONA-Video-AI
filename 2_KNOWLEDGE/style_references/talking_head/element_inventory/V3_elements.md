## 1. CARD / PANEL (the dominant vocabulary of this reel)

### 1.1 Top "Topic Banner" card (hook + section title) — PERSISTS per section
- **name/desc:** Rounded dark pill/plaque anchored top-center holding the current section title in ALL-CAPS (e.g. "API KEY CHO HERMES", "API KEY FREE CHO HERMES", "TRẢ PHÍ = TỐN KÉM", "MODEL FREE #1", "DÙNG CHO HERMES"). Has a small subheading line in muted grey beneath the caps title.
- **category:** card/panel
- **visual style:** Rounded-rectangle / lozenge, dark translucent glassy fill (near-black with slight blue tint), thin neon/cyan hairline border + soft outer glow. Title text white bold caps; sub-line small grey. A tiny accent glyph/arrow sometimes sits left of the title. ~70% width, top ~8% of frame.
- **concept it illustrates:** "what this segment is about" — the running chapter/topic label.
- **position zone:** top-center band.
- **entrance/animation:** fades/pops in at each section change, holds, swaps on cut.
- **timing/sync + frequency:** PERSISTS through each section; SWAPS ~8-10 times across the reel (one per topic/step block). Accumulating theme count ~8-10 distinct titles.
- **asset type needed:** CSS-drawn rounded panel (glass fill + hairline stroke + glow) + dynamic caps title text + sub-line text.
- **SEOSONA remap:** panel → white/very-light card with soft blue hairline + soft drop-shadow (no neon). Title dark ink; sub-line grey-blue. Accent glyph = brand blue. Neon glow → soft shadow.

- **SEOSONA remap:** light card, blue "STEP N" number chip, coral or amber accent for the active dot; grey inactive dots; soft shadow instead of glow.
