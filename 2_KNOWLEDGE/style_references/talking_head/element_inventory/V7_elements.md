## Repetition summary
- **T1 two-tone caption band** — the workhorse; present on ~15+ frames, one per spoken beat (accumulates words, swaps per line).
- **D1 green neon glow + D4 vignette + D3 gloss** — persistent environmental decorative layer across the whole reel.
- **Floating deliverable-as-object** pattern repeats 4×: phone (M1) → Raw tiles (M2) → folder (I1) → course book (M3) — each is a physical stand-in for an intangible thing you receive.
- **Green highlight ring (G1)** — once, but it's the visual "proof selector" motif and pairs with the green brand accent used throughout captions.
- **Label chips (C1)** ×3 under Raw tiles; meta plates (C2, C3) once each.

## Signature elements (the 3–5 that define this reel)
1. **Deliverable-as-floating-object tiles** — phone mockup, "Raw 1/2/3" thumbnail tiles, 3D folder, and upright course booklet, all levitating over the open palm with soft shadows. This "hold the intangible in your hand" motif is the reel's identity. *(Assets: phone-bezel PNG, real screenshot/footage-thumb fills, filled folder icon PNG, 3D card/booklet mockup.)*
2. **Two-tone kinetic caption band (T1)** — bold ALL-CAPS VN with the key word popped in **emerald-green**, synced word-by-word to VO. *(Asset: CSS/ASS styled per-word colored text.)*
3. **Emerald neon glow environment (D1)** — the green rim-light + radial bloom on near-black that ties every frame together. *(Asset: CSS radial-gradient/glow layers + real lighting.)*
4. **Green highlight ring / proof selector (G1)** — the glowing green outline framing the DM screenshot as "here's the proof." *(Asset: SVG rounded-rect stroke + soft glow.)*
5. **Chat-proof phone mockup (M1)** — real client-DM screenshots inside a glossy tilting iPhone bezel with light-sweep, opening the reel with social proof. *(Asset: phone-bezel PNG + real screenshot.)*

**Light-brand takeaway:** the whole look is neon-on-black; the SEOSONA remap keeps the *structure* (floating objects, soft shadows, two-tone captions, proof rings) but swaps neon-green → brand roles (**blue** primary, **coral** CTA, **green** = proof/free, **amber** = the folder/quantity accent) and replaces every neon glow with a soft light-grey drop shadow on a light background.
