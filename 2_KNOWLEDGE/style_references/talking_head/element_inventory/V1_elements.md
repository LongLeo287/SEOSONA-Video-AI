# V1 Element Inventory — "10 Bộ Preset của Khánh"

**Source reel:** V1 "10 Bộ Preset của Khánh" (~32s, 9:16 talking-head, video-editing/preset-share niche)
**Contact sheet:** `8_WORKSPACE/talking_head_study/elements/sheets/01_10_Bộ_Preset_của_Khánh_...jpg` (23 keyframes)
**Scope:** every small visual element LAYERED over footage — icons, emojis, stickers, images, cards/panels, text-effects, cursors, decoratives. The 3-zone layout itself is documented elsewhere and excluded here.

**Palette of the reel:** dark studio footage (neutral grey hoodie, dark room, blue/teal monitor glow behind) + a signature **gold/amber-yellow** graphic accent + **coral-orange** text highlights + **white** body text + occasional **red** notification dot. Neon glow rims on floating cards. Warm cream/off-white full-bleed graphic backgrounds on transition frames.

## CATEGORY: card / panel

### CP-1 · Floating "phone-screen" photo card (glass slab)
- **Category:** card/panel
- **Visual style:** tall rounded-rectangle slab framed like a phone screen; glass/translucent fill with a bright **white neon glow rim**; holds a photographic still inside (person standing / road scene). Soft drop shadow, slight 3D tilt/float. Medium size, center-frame overlapping the presenter.
- **Concept:** "here are examples of edits / other videos" — showcases sample footage the presets apply to.
- **Position zone:** center / mid-frame, overlapping presenter's chest & mic.
- **Entrance/animation:** floats/scales in with glow bloom; subtle bob; cross-dissolves out.
- **Timing/sync + frequency:** frames #1–#2 (~0.7–2.1s) opener. **Accumulates** (stacked with badges). ~1–2 instances.
- **Asset to reproduce:** CSS-drawn rounded-rect card (glass) + inner **real image / screenshot** slot + phone-bezel-lite frame.
- **SEOSONA remap:** rim glow → **soft blue drop-shadow**; glass tint stays light; frame = brand blue 1px stroke, keep light background. Photo slot = real thumbnail.

### CP-2 · "10 BỘ PRESET / KHÁNH HAY DÙNG" preset-list receipt card
- **Category:** card/panel
- **Visual style:** dark charcoal near-black **paper/receipt** card with a **curled / peeling corner** (torn-paper 3D), rounded, soft shadow; header "10 BỘ PRESET" + subhead; body = long list of ~15–20 rows each with a name + right-aligned timestamp/number. Monospace-ish list. Large, right-of-center.
- **Concept:** the actual deliverable — the list of 10 preset packs with counts.
- **Position zone:** center-right, large; on frame #4 dark bg, frame #5 cream bg.
- **Entrance/animation:** slides/drops in with the peel corner catching light; holds; parallax.
- **Timing/sync:** frames #4–#5 (~5.0–6.4s). **Persists** across 2 frames. ~1 instance (2 bg variants).
- **Asset to reproduce:** CSS-drawn card with a **peel-corner** shape (SVG mask) + data-driven list rows (name + value).
- **SEOSONA remap:** dark card → **light card, ink-on-cream**; peel corner shadow → soft grey; row values in brand blue; header in coral. Keep light.

### CP-3 · Software UI screenshot panel — effect/preset menu list
- **Category:** card/panel (screenshot)
- **Visual style:** dark editing-app dropdown/menu screenshot: rows of effect names (Image – Creative, Special Effects, Transitions – Dissolve/Wipe, X-Blur, X-Chip, X-Drip Glow, X-Hand Draw, X-Shadow Studio, X-Text Source, X-Text zoom, V-Wiggle…) with small right-side value labels. Tiny UI type. Framed as inset panel over cream bg.
- **Concept:** proof the presets are real / shows what's inside the pack.
- **Position zone:** center-right inset over graphic bg (frames #6–#7); full software UI (#18).
- **Entrance/animation:** slides up / cross-cut; static hold.
- **Timing/sync:** #6–#7 (~7.5–9.3s) and #18 (~25s After-Effects timeline). **Accumulates** ~3 instances.
- **Asset to reproduce:** **real screenshot** of editing software (cropped) inside a card frame.
- **SEOSONA remap:** keep screenshot as-is (real product), frame border → brand blue thin stroke, drop neon; caption in brand type.

### CP-4 · Gold embossed metal card / plate
- **Category:** card/panel (decorative hero)
- **Visual style:** metallic **gold** rounded-rect plate with embossed/engraved detail, sitting on a **ribbed/corrugated gold** full-bleed background; specular highlights, luxe finish. Large, centered.
- **Concept:** premium "gift / value" beat — signals the preset pack is a valuable free gift.
- **Position zone:** center, full-bleed gold scene.
- **Entrance/animation:** light-sweep / shimmer reveal.
- **Timing/sync:** frame #17 (~23.6s). Transition/hero beat. ~1 instance.
- **Asset to reproduce:** CSS gradient plate + noise/emboss OR a **rendered gold-card image**; ribbed bg via repeating-linear-gradient.
- **SEOSONA remap:** gold luxe → **amber-role accent card on cream**, flatten metal to soft gradient; keep light, no heavy specular; use amber sparingly as the "reward" role.

### CP-5 · Halftone / scanline portrait card
- **Category:** card/panel (stylized image)
- **Visual style:** presenter's face rendered as a **gold halftone / vertical-scanline** duotone on a saturated gold field; retro-print texture; overlaid text.
- **Concept:** stylized "hope these presets help you" closing sentiment beat.
- **Position zone:** center, full-bleed gold.
- **Entrance/animation:** scanline wipe / glitch-in.
- **Timing/sync:** frame #19 (~26.5s). ~1 instance.
- **Asset to reproduce:** **photo-cutout** run through halftone/scanline shader (SVG filter or pre-rendered PNG).
- **SEOSONA remap:** gold duotone → **blue-or-amber duotone on cream**; scanlines soft, low-contrast; keep light.

### CP-6 · Small inner text-card labels ("SLAY SOCIAL", "SS.31", "IF YOU'RE ABOUT TO INVEST")
- **Category:** card/panel (label chips inside CP-1)
- **Visual style:** small pill/label text baked onto the sample photos — bold caption stickers ("SLAY SOCIAL", "SS.31", "IF YOU'RE ABOUT TO INVEST!!"). White/bold on the photo.
- **Concept:** mimics real short-form captions on the sample clips.

- **SEOSONA remap:** brand caption style (coral highlight word), keep light.

### TX-1 · Lower-third 2-color highlight caption (PRIMARY motif)
- **Category:** text-effect
- **Visual style:** bold uppercase Vietnamese lower-third, **two-tone**: most words **white**, the emphasis word(s) in **coral-orange or gold-yellow**. Heavy weight, tight tracking, subtle dark shadow/edge for legibility. Sometimes 2 lines.
- **Concept:** spoken-word emphasis / readability captions (e.g. "THÌ HÔM NAY / KHÁNH SẼ", "CHO NÊN LÀ KHÁNH / MUỐN CHIA SẺ", "FOLLOW VÀ / COMMENT", "CẢM ƠN MỌI NGƯỜI / RẤT NHIỀU!").
- **Position zone:** lower-third, centered.

- **Timing/sync:** **persists & accumulates** across nearly every frame (#2, #8–#16, #20–#23). Highest-frequency element, ~16+ distinct lines.
- **Asset:** CSS/kinetic type; two color spans; drop-shadow.
- **SEOSONA remap:** white base + **coral** highlight word (primary) / **amber** for reward beats; soft shadow not glow; keep light-legible.

## SIGNATURE ELEMENTS (the 3–5 that define this reel's look)
1. **TX-1 — two-tone highlight lower-third caption** (white body + coral/gold emphasis word). The workhorse; sets the entire read.
2. **Gold/amber accent system** — giant "10" (TX-2), gold plate (CP-4), halftone portrait (CP-5), diagonal swipe (DEC-1), ribbed bg (DEC-2). A cohesive luxe-gold "value/gift" motif for intro+outro.
3. **TX-3 — "PRESET" in a dashed selection-box with transform handles.** The editing-software gag that ties the graphics to the niche.
4. **CP-2 — dark peel-corner receipt card** listing the 10 presets (the deliverable's hero shot).
5. **CP-1 + DEC-4 — glowing floating glass phone-cards** with sample footage (the opener hook).
