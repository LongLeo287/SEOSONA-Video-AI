# Marketing Psychology — persuasion levers for a short video

A short video has ~2s to hook and ~4s to close. You can't use many levers — pick **ONE lever for the
HOOK** and **ONE for the CTA**, and make the whole script serve them. Below are the highest-leverage
principles from the full 78-item library, kept only if they work inside 45–60 seconds of faceless SEO/
marketing video. Each is a *tool for honest persuasion* — never fabricate the number/proof it leverages
(the traceability gate will reject invented data).

## HOOK levers (open the loop in the first 2 seconds)
- **Curiosity Gap** — state that a known outcome has a *hidden* cause/method the viewer doesn't have yet.
  ("Lý do SEO của bạn không lên top KHÔNG phải do từ khoá.")
- **Zeigarnik Effect (open loop)** — pose an unfinished thought the brain wants closed; pay it off only at
  the end. Great for retention.
- **Loss Aversion / Framing** — frame the cost of NOT acting ("Mỗi ngày chậm SEO = đối thủ lấy mất khách").
  Losses hit ~2× harder than equivalent gains.
- **Pratfall Effect** — admit one honest limitation up front ("Cách này không nhanh, nhưng…") → trust.
- **Availability Heuristic** — lead with a concrete, imaginable case so the outcome feels achievable.

## BODY levers (hold attention + build belief)
- **Social Proof / Bandwagon** — real counts/logos/reviews ("X doanh nghiệp đã dùng"). Only REAL numbers.
- **Anchoring + Contrast** — set a reference first, then show your point against it ("Thay vì 6 tháng, chỉ 6 tuần").
- **Jobs-to-Be-Done** — frame around the job, not the feature ("Người ta không cần công cụ SEO — cần khách hàng").
- **Goal-Gradient Effect** — show progress ("3 bước, bạn đã xong bước 1") to pull the viewer forward.
- **Mere Exposure** — consistent brand block every video builds preference over time (why the HERO opener + logo matter).

## CTA levers (close in the last 3–5 seconds)
- **Peak-End Rule** — the ending is over-weighted in memory: end on the single strongest line + a clean CTA.
- **Scarcity** — genuine limits only (a real deadline/quota). Never fake urgency.
- **Reciprocity** — give value first (the tip WAS the value) → the follow ask feels earned.
- **Commitment & Consistency** — ask for a tiny next step ("Lưu lại video này") before the bigger one.
- **Zero-Price Effect** — "free" has outsized pull; if something is genuinely free, say it plainly.

## How the pipeline uses this
- `script_writer.reason()` picks the ANGLE **and one HOOK lever**; `write()` bakes it into scene 0.
- The CTA scene picks one CTA lever (default: Peak-End + Reciprocity).
- Ethics rule: every lever must ride on a REAL fact/number from KeyFacts — no invented scarcity/proof.
