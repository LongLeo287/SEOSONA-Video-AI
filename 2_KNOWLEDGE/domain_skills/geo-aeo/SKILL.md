# GEO / AEO — optimizing to be CITED by AI answers

**The shift:** classic SEO wins a ranked link on a results page. **GEO/AEO wins a CITATION inside the
AI's answer** (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews). Users increasingly read the AI answer
and never click a blue link — so if the AI doesn't cite you, you're invisible even when you rank #1.

## Why it matters now (real 2026 signals — cite these in videos)
- **Google AI Overviews** appear on a large and growing share of queries (~half of informational searches).
- **Wikipedia ≈ the single most-cited source** in ChatGPT answers (~48%) → structured, factual, entity-clear
  content wins citations; thin marketing pages don't.
- **LLM-referred traffic converts far higher** than classic search (reported ~6× in some studies) — fewer
  visitors, but they arrive pre-qualified by the AI's recommendation.
- Success metric changes from *rank position* → **citation frequency + share-of-voice in AI answers**.

## How to WIN citations (the playbook)
1. **Answer the question directly, up top.** Lead with a 2–3 sentence direct answer (the "answer-first"
   inverted pyramid) — AI extractors quote the concise, self-contained passage.
2. **Be chunk-ready for RAG.** Short, self-contained sections with clear H2/H3 headings; each section
   answerable on its own (the AI retrieves passages, not whole pages).
3. **Entity clarity + structured data.** Schema (FAQ, HowTo, Article, Organization) + consistent entity
   naming so the model knows who/what you are. This is where our `schema-markup` skill plugs in.
4. **Earn trust signals.** Citations × Trust = share-of-voice: real author/expertise, sources, freshness,
   consistency across the web (the model triangulates you against Wikipedia/authoritative sources).
5. **`llms.txt` + `/.well-known/ai.txt`.** A root file that tells AI crawlers what your site is and which
   pages matter — an emerging convention (like robots.txt for LLMs). Set crawler tiers in robots.txt.
6. **Mine the questions.** Harvest 150–300 real questions your audience asks an AI, then publish the crisp
   answer to each — question-shaped content is what answer-engines retrieve.

## GEO vs classic SEO (the video framing)
| | Classic SEO | GEO / AEO |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | rank a link | be cited in the AI answer |
| Unit | page / keyword | passage / entity / question |
| Metric | position, CTR | citation frequency, share-of-voice |
| Content shape | long, keyword-dense | answer-first, chunked, structured |
| Trust | backlinks | entity consistency + authoritative corroboration |
