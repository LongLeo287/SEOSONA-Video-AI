## Scope & method

- **Source dir:** `D:\SEOSONA AI\data\Video Template`
- **Slice:** files at sorted index **29–42** (`ls | LC_ALL=C sort`), 14 files. All **1080×1920 @30fps** except v42 (**720×1280**).
- **Method:** `ffprobe` durations; `scdet` hard-cut detection (thr 0.3); **contact-sheet montages @ 1 frame / 2s** (6×8 tiles, tile *k* = `2·k` s, page-2 starts at 96 s) read as images; plus **full-res hero frames** pulled at named timestamps for typography/hex on the highest-priority templates (v29, v31, v33, v36, v41).
- **Frame-limit honesty:** montages sample every **2 s**, so sub-2s motion (entrance eases, motion-blur, per-word caption cadence) is **inferred** from adjacent tiles + the few full-res grabs, not measured frame-accurately. Exact easing curves / durations below are **estimates** flagged as such. Hard-cut lists are exact. Colour hexes are eyeballed from full-res frames (±10).

## HEADLINE FINDING — this slice is ~2 template engines, not 14 hand-crafts

The reference set is **machine-generated by a handful of "URL→video" tools**. Extract the
**template grammar**, not surface decoration:

- **YupVid.com** — 4/14 (v29, v32, v35, v38). Dark theme, **accent is themeable** (red on v29/v32/v38, **green on v35** — the green variant is closest to us). Heavy on screen-recordings + AI-generated full-bleed imagery + phone/isometric mockups. Outro watermark "yupvid.com" / "Xem thêm" / a "CERTIFIED" seal.
- **escbase.xyz** — 4/14 (v31 cyan, v33-adjacent, v36 blue, v42 green). Fully **self-drawn** motion-graphics (gauges, bar charts, pipeline cards) — **the strongest model for our engine**. Crucially, escbase **auto-themes its single accent to the subject's brand colour** (Kimi=cyan, Meta=blue, NVIDIA=green). We are brand-LOCKED, so we fix the accent — but the "one accent drives every highlight/node/gauge/keyword" discipline is exactly right. Outro: "Template Tạo Video Viral — Biến một URL thành video — escbase.xyz".
- **trạm ai "SPATIAL FLOW"** (v33) — a camera-travels-a-path node-journey template.
- **@aidailyone** ships both a dark **kinetic-caption** style (v40) and a **light neo-brutalist** style (v41 — the ONLY light-themed video in the slice, structurally closest to our brand).
- Remainder (v30, v34, v39) are simpler narrative/spotlight-card styles.

**Implication for rebuild:** the whole slice reduces to a small set of reusable *scene archetypes* (title, stat-hero, gauge, bar-chart, pipeline/flow, feature-list, comparison, quote, code/terminal, outro-summary) + one caption system (per-word keyword highlight). Build those archetypes brand-locked and we match the entire corpus.
