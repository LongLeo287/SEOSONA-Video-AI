## 3–5 DISTINCT reusable craft patterns across the 7
1. **Persistent section-furniture stack (top) + karaoke caption (bottom), face-clean middle.**
   Video 2's chapter-pill + two-line heading + emoji takeaway-chip is a complete listicle
   template; the top-28% / bottom-20% furniture split with an empty face band is the safe-zone
   backbone for long-form talking heads.
2. **Word-level karaoke with a single accent color, three legibility strategies.** Same core
   idea, three executions to copy per context: no-box + heavy shadow (V1 line-level mint,
   V2 word-level orange) vs. dark **frosted scrim box** (V3 yellow/green) — box when the
   background is busy/RGB, shadow-only when clean.
3. **Shrink-speaker-to-inset + card-above (reflow safe-zone).** Video 3's move — when a graphic
   panel appears, physically resize the speaker into a rounded inset and place the card in the
   vacated space, so graphics never overlap the face. Superior to static offsetting.
4. **Structured info graphics: icon-tile bulleted panel & animated numbered checklist with
   progress bar.** Video 3's corner-bracket panel (per-row icon chips) and self-advancing
   checklist are directly portable "explain-a-process" components.
5. **Topic-matched object stickers + staggered 1–2 word kinetic keywords (+ optional AR-HUD /
   selection-handle motif).** Videos 4–7's grammar: oversized offset two-line keyword, floating
   icons that literally depict the topic, and gimmick overlays (Figma selection handles, tracking
   reticles) — a fast kinetic style for short cutaways/hooks.
